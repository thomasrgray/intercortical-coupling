# GC response structure

## Panels

A: responsive fractions and latency by route. B: GC rasters/PSTHs. C: evidence for or against three phases in retro. D: corresponding ortho dynamics.

## Required inputs

GC units, event/alignment table and population model outputs.

## Interpretation and validation

Three phases are a hypothesis. Compare model evidence and held-out prediction; do not force a three-state result.

## Output contract

Save panel PNG/PDF, plotted values, counts/exclusions, configuration and source revision under `results/<run>/fig05_gc_dynamics/`. Panel letters are provisional. No synthetic output may be labeled as study data.
